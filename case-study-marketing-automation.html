Marketing Automation Platform

A campaign platform that replaced manual email and SMS sends with scheduling, templates, and real performance tracking.

Client
PulseReach Marketing
Industry
Marketing technology
Timeline
12 weeks
Services
Product design, Desktop development

The challenge

Every campaign was a manual, one-off effort.

PulseReach's team was sending email and SMS campaigns manually, with no shared templates and no reliable way to see what was actually working after launch.

Research

Auditing where every campaign hour was actually going.

We tracked the full lifecycle of five recent campaigns end to end, and benchmarked three competing automation tools, to find exactly where manual effort could become reusable structure.

Strategy & design

Templates, scheduling, and one dashboard for performance.

We designed a desktop application with a visual campaign builder, reusable templates, automated scheduling, and a single performance dashboard covering both email and SMS.
