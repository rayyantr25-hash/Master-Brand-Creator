Online Retail Marketplace

A scalable marketplace rebuild that made onboarding new vendors and managing products far less painful.

Client
GATBI
Industry
Multi-vendor retail marketplace
Timeline
14 weeks
Services
Web design, Web development

The challenge

Onboarding a new vendor took days, not minutes.

GATBI's marketplace was struggling to scale — vendor onboarding was a manual, multi-day process, and product management tools hadn't kept pace with catalog growth.

Research

Walking the vendor onboarding flow and the buyer journey end to end.

We walked through the full vendor onboarding flow and mapped the buyer journey from search to checkout, to find where friction was costing the most time and sales.

Strategy & design

A rebuilt architecture, designed to scale with the vendor list.

We rebuilt the marketplace on a more scalable architecture with a streamlined vendor onboarding flow and simplified product management, without disrupting live buyer traffic.
